Thermographic Inspection Services

Thermographic inspections help reduce costly breakdowns and prevent damage by finding equipment failures in their early stages.

Thermography produces visible images from invisible thermal radiation. Infrared cameras detect thermal energy and convert it into an image, which a trained thermographer then analyses to identify any problem areas.

Thermographic Surveys

Thermographic surveys of small to medium-sized factories in the UK can often be completed in a single day.

Our inspections focus on distribution boards, switchgear and related equipment, using quality survey equipment including a handheld thermal imager.

You receive a comprehensive report with both digital and infrared images, a detailed log of every fault found, and prioritised actions to reduce costly maintenance and protect productivity.
